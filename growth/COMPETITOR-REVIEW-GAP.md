# Competitor review gap — Mills Electric vs. the Brockton market

**All figures checked 2026-10-21.** Every number carries its source URL. Google counts
and non-Google counts are labeled separately and are never added together.

**This is the exhibit.** If the owner reads one page in this folder, make it this one.

---

## How these numbers were obtained, and what that limits

Google Maps, Google Search, Yelp, Angi and HomeAdvisor all return 403 / 429 / CAPTCHA to
automated fetching. **I never saw the live Google local pack.** So "top 8" below means
*the eight highest-review-volume electricians serving Brockton and its eight town-page
towns that I could verify*, not an observed ranking order.

The counts themselves are solid. Birdeye's business pages print a **per-source
breakdown** — "Google (84)", "Google: 38 reviews, Birdeye: 0 reviews" — which is the one
free source that isolates Google from everything else. The top two entries were fetched
twice, independently, and matched.

Where a figure is not confirmed as Google-specific, it says so. **UNVERIFIED** means
exactly that.

## The number

**Mills Electric needs 18 more Google reviews to reach the competitor median of 22.**

That is the whole ask. Not a campaign, not a budget — eighteen customers, out of the
hundreds Mills has served since 1979, saying something true about work already done and
already paid for. At four reviews earned in roughly six years of the profile existing,
Mills has been getting about **0.7 reviews a year by accident.** Asking deliberately,
even at a modest conversion, closes an 18-review gap in a single season: a contractor
doing three jobs a week who asks every one and converts a quarter of them clears 18 in
about ten weeks.

Against the top three the arithmetic runs: **+35 to pass John Page Electric (38)** — the
only Brockton-proper competitor with real volume, and the single most valuable target
because the map pack for "electrician Brockton MA" is decided inside the city; **+43 to
pass Suburban Electric (46)**; **+81 to pass Averill Electric (84)**, which is a Holbrook
company and a two-year project, not a ninety-day one.

The near-term target is not Averill. **It is 22 reviews to reach the median, and 39 to
take the top spot in Brockton itself.** The second number is the one that moves the
phone, because Mills already beats John Page on rating (5.0 vs 4.9) and would, at 39
reviews, beat them on both.

## Two things that make the gap smaller than it looks

1. **Brockton proper is a soft market.** Strip out the suburbs and the in-city field is
   John Page (38), Landerholm (8), WS Anderson (6, rated 3.8) and Brava Electric (5, and
   [BBB indicates no longer in business](https://www.bbb.org/us/ma/brockton/category/electrician)).
   The median *inside Brockton* is about 7. **Mills passes Landerholm and WS Anderson —
   moves from last in the city to second — at roughly 9 reviews.** That is five asks.

2. **Mills' rating is a genuine asset, not a vanity stat.** Suburban Electric has 46
   reviews at 4.3. Mills at 22 reviews and 5.0 would look better to a homeowner reading
   both than Suburban does, because a clean 5.0 with real volume behind it is the
   strongest combination on this page.

## One thing that makes it bigger

**[Kuhlman Electric](https://www.kuhlmanelectricalservices.com/service-areas/brockton-ma)**
is not a Brockton company — it operates out of the North Shore — but it runs a Brockton
service-area page and advertises "2,500+ Five-Star Reviews" (a self-reported
cross-platform total; its Google count specifically is **UNVERIFIED**). It is running the
same service-area-page strategy as Mills' eight town pages, at national scale. Mills will
not out-review it. Mills beats it the way a local contractor always beats a franchise —
on being actually, verifiably local, with a licence number, a 1979 start date and real
Brockton names in the reviews. **That makes the review count matter more, not less.**

## Caveats, stated plainly

- **No observed Google SERP.** Ranking order above is inferred from directory prominence
  and review volume, not from the live local pack. The *counts* are verified; the
  *ordering as Google would show it* is not.
- Birdeye search snippets lag its own live pages (John Page showed 29 → 37 → 38 across
  snippet, directory and business page). **Live business-page numbers were used
  throughout.** All counts will drift; re-check before quoting these in three months.
- Expertise.com's Google figures are stale — it credits Michael Post Electric with 2
  Google reviews while showing 48 on Facebook. **Not used as a source for any figure in
  the main table.**
- Several nearby firms have meaningful Angi/Yelp volume but **UNVERIFIED** Google counts:
  Dever Electric (South Easton, Angi 5.0/51–59), Champion Electrical (Angi 5.0/59),
  South Shore Power Solutions (Angi 4.9/35), DoCanto Electrical (Yelp 4.6/16).
  **If their Google counts are comparable to their Angi counts, the median rises.** The
  22 figure should be read as a floor.
