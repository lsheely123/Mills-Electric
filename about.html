About us

Who you are
calling

A small crew of licensed electricians working out of Brockton. The same people quote the job, do the work, and meet the inspector.

How we got here

Mills Electric started in 1979 with one van and a licence. We have stayed deliberately small since — big enough to take on a full service upgrade, small enough that the person who quotes your job is the person who shows up to do it.

Most of our work comes from neighbours telling neighbours. That only holds if the work is right, so we would rather turn down a job than rush it.

How we work

We quote in writing with parts and labour separated. We pull the permit, we meet the inspector, and we hand you the closed-out paperwork. If something we installed fails inside the warranty, we come back and fix it at no charge.
