What Holbrook is built out of

Holbrook is a small town with modest housing stock: mostly capes and ranches from the 1950s build-out, on small lots, with some older homes closer to the centre. There is not a lot of new construction, which means a high proportion of houses are still working off something close to their original electrical service.

In practice that is a 100-amp panel if somebody upgraded it at some point, or a 60-amp fuse box if nobody ever did. Both are common here, and the fuse boxes are the ones that tend to surface when a house changes hands or an insurer starts asking questions.

Holbrook specifics

The finished attic on one circuit

The classic Holbrook cape has a second floor that was finished after the fact, and almost always off a single circuit run up from below. One bedroom, sometimes two, plus whatever lighting — all on one 15-amp branch that was never intended to be anybody's living space. It works until someone puts a window air conditioner or a space heater up there, and then it does not.

The fix is proper circuits for those rooms, which usually means spare capacity in the panel, which on a 60-amp fuse service means the service first. We will tell you the order the work has to happen in and what each stage costs, so you can do it in pieces if that suits you better.

The other recurring Holbrook call is storm damage. Heavy tree cover over overhead service drops means a branch takes the weatherhead or bends the mast. That is an emergency call and we answer those any hour.
