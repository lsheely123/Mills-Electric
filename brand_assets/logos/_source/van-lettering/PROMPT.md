# Van lettering → clean logo (Higgsfield)

Goal: recreate the gold lettering on the side of the Mills van as a flat logo —
radial brushed (engine-turned) gold leaf over a solid dark red drop shadow, on black.

- Reference image: `reference-van-lettering.png` (tight crop of `assets/mills-van.jpg`)
- Leaves out the van's "Masters LIC. #11245A" line (site uses #A-12245, settled 2026-11-02).
- Run via the Higgsfield connector, or `make_van_logo.py` with HF_KEY in the environment.
- Output is raster: fine for web/screens; a wrap or sign still needs a vector trace.

## Prompt
Recreate the gold vehicle lettering in this reference photo as a clean, flat, perfectly front-facing logo on a plain solid black background. No van, no window, no reflections, no perspective, no photo texture. Keep the exact wording, layout and bold sans-serif type style of the reference: the word 'Mills' on the first line with a long gold horizontal rule to its left and right; 'Electric' large on the second line with a small vertically stacked 'Inc.' to its right; then 'Brockton, MA' and '586-2955' on one line; then 'ALARM SYSTEMS' with a gold rule on each side. Do not include the 'Masters LIC.' line. Every letter and rule is radial brushed gold leaf (engine-turned gold: rows of small circular brushed swirls with a bright metallic sheen), with a solid dark red drop shadow offset down and to the left behind every letter and rule, exactly like the reference. Crisp, sharp edges, centred, high resolution, sign-painter quality, spelling exactly as given.
